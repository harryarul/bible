Overview

The Preacher searches everything — pleasure, work, wealth, wisdom — and finds it all ‘vanity,’ a vapour that slips through the fingers. Life ‘under the sun’ is fleeting and often unfair.

Yet the conclusion is not despair but humble faith: enjoy God’s good gifts, fear Him, and keep His commands. Only God gives lasting meaning to a fleeting life.

Key Themes

The fleeting nature of lifeLimits of human wisdom and wealthEnjoying God’s gifts as giftsFear God and keep His commands

Key Verse

“To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven.”

Pointing to Christ

Ecclesiastes exposes the emptiness of life apart from God, preparing the heart for the One who gives life that is abundant and eternal (John 10:10).
