Chapter Eight

Resources

“Study to show thyself approved unto God.” — 2 Timothy 2:15 (KJV)

❖ ❖ ❖

All the references throughout this site come from the New Testament — chiefly the book of Acts and Paul’s own letters. The links below point to free, public resources where you can read the full text and study Paul more deeply. They open in a new tab.

I Read the Text

  • Bible Gateway — Acts 9 (the conversion) ↗Read any passage in dozens of translations and languages.
  • Bible Gateway — Romans ↗Paul’s fullest statement of the gospel.
  • Bible Study Tools — the book of Acts ↗The narrative of Paul’s journeys, with commentaries.
  • YouVersion (Bible.com) ↗Free reading plans, audio Bible, and apps.
  • Blue Letter Bible ↗Greek/Hebrew word study tools and concordances.

II Study & Background

  • Encyclopaedia Britannica — Saint Paul the Apostle ↗A scholarly overview of his life, dates, and influence.
  • Bible Odyssey (SBL) ↗Articles by biblical scholars on Paul and his world.
  • Biblical Archaeology Society ↗Archaeology of the cities Paul visited.
  • Catholic Encyclopedia — St. Paul ↗Detailed entry covering tradition of his death and burial.

III Early Sources on His Death

  • Clement of Rome — 1 Clement ↗One of the earliest references to Paul’s martyrdom (late 1st century).
  • Eusebius — Church History ↗Records the tradition of Paul’s beheading under Nero.
How to use this site. Each chapter’s scripture citations (like Acts 9:15 or Philippians 1:21) can be looked up directly on Bible Gateway or Blue Letter Bible by typing the reference into their search box. The Letters page links each epistle to its full text.
