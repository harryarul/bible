The Studio

Good design disappears. I sweat the micro-decisions — the timing of a transition, the weight of a word, the moment a tap becomes trust.

For eight years I've helped founders and product teams turn fuzzy ambition into interfaces people reach for daily. I work end-to-end: strategy and research through to the production-ready front-end, because the magic usually lives in the handoff nobody owns.

Previously design lead at two venture-backed startups; now independent, taking on a handful of partners a year.
