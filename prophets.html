The Prophets

Men called out of fields, palaces and exile to speak for God — to warn, to comfort, and to point ahead to the coming Messiah. Tap any prophet to walk through their life, their message, the heart of their book, and the scriptures themselves.

What is a prophet?

A prophet (Hebrew nabi) was God's spokesman — someone who received His word and delivered it without softening it. They were watchmen calling a wandering nation back to covenant faithfulness, often at great personal cost. Some left written books; others (like Elijah) thundered in action rather than ink.

Their messages followed a rhythm you'll see again and again: You have broken the covenant → judgment is coming → yet God will keep His promise and restore a remnant → and One is coming who will make all things right.
